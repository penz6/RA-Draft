# 2026–2027 deadline sources

`deadlines.json` was transcribed from the seven supplied RA curriculum PDFs on
October 19, 2026. Only assignment deadlines and explicit staff commitments on or
after that date are included. Fall dates use 2026 and spring dates use 2027.
No time was invented where a source supplied only a date; those entries remain
due through the end of the day in America/New_York.

Each entry includes its source filename and PDF page number. The PDFs themselves
are not stored in this repository. Submission links come from the guides.
Community Meeting and HawkTalk completion/reflection deadlines are combined
where both are due on the same day. Activity windows are preserved in descriptions.
Building-wide CBA dates for Willow, Stonewall, and North were removed at the
user’s request. The individual Activity/CBA assignment remains included. The assignment tracker says dates are subject to change.

## Source conflicts and exclusions

- Fall Explorations activity 1: the user confirmed on October 19, 2026 that this
  deadline has already passed. It remains excluded from the upcoming feed.
  The detailed guide (page 1) lists September 30, while the assignment tracker
  lists October 30; the tracker date is not used to create an upcoming deadline.
- Winter closing: the tracker lists December 4–12, while the Bulletin Boards
  guide (page 7) says residence halls close December 17 at 9 a.m. These could
  refer to different processes, but the PDFs do not establish that. Closing
  dates are excluded pending confirmation.
- Some guide weekday labels do not match the 2026–2027 calendar (for example,
  the January 18 reopening is called Sunday). The academic-year heading and
  explicit month/day determine the assignment dates; weekday labels are not
  used to change them.
- Resident move-out/reopening dates, guest-freeze periods, awareness days,
  resource publication dates, and example dates are not RA assignment deadlines
  and are not imported. Rolling requirements without a specific deadline are
  also omitted.
- All dates before October 19, 2026 are excluded, including the November board's
  October 15 proposal deadline and the first fall HawkTalk deadline. No overdue
  deadline has been moved forward or repeated in a new year.

Edit `deadlines.json` when a supervisor confirms a change. Maintain the
`source` fields when correcting or adding deadlines. The UI automatically hides
deadlines after they pass; removing old entries from the file later is optional.
