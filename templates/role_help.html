How RA Duty Picking works

{% if me.role == 'RA' %}

RA permissions

  • Choose a building once on first sign-in; an Admin can correct it later.
  • See sessions and the live assignment calendar for that building.
  • Wait for your turn. The order repeats until all duty slots are filled.
  • When your turn opens, click an available calendar date and confirm it.
  • Download your own event or the full session iCalendar file.

RAs cannot change the order, assign other people, skip turns, or alter capacity.

{% elif me.role == 'HRA' %}

HRA permissions

  • Everything an RA can do, including picking when included in a session.
  • Create sessions for the assigned building and drag participants into turn order.
  • Pick a date for any participant directly from the turn list and calendar.
  • Skip one turn, pause or restore future turns, and remove incorrect assignments.
  • Choose weekday-first, any-date, or weekend-first selection and override individual date capacity.
  • Treat edge-case dates as weekdays or weekends, or mark dates as no one needed.
  • Close or reopen sessions and download the full schedule.

HRAs cannot manage another building or change user roles.

{% else %}

Admin permissions

  • Everything an HRA can do, across every building.
  • Pick normally when included in a session.
  • Create buildings and assign user roles and buildings.
  • Manage every turn, assignment, capacity override, date treatment, and session state.
  • Review the audit log for administrative and scheduling changes.
{% endif %}
